Pass if the answer does ONE of two things well: (a) opens a bounded questioning round — at most about 5 numbered questions, scope among them, each carrying a recommended answer the user could accept as-is; or (b) proceeds to design or build and states the assumptions it made. Fail if it asks questions without recommendations, asks more than about 5 at once, or asks the user to decide without either committing to a recommendation or proceeding.
